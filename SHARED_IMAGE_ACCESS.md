# Shared Image Access for a Private Client
*Phillip J. Pirages Fine Books & Manuscripts*

I established and maintained a Dropbox folder through which a private client and his office could examine photographs of books and manuscripts at their convenience.

The material included photographs taken in our office and images supplied by other sellers. I organized the files into folders for particular items or groups, making complete available image sets accessible for review and download. Some folders also contained supporting descriptions and documents.

Sharing a folder link provided a practical alternative to sending numerous large email attachments. Once the arrangement was established, I communicated directly with the client about the available material.

My contribution combined photography, file preparation, organization, and client support. The surviving folder contains work from several sources and includes material added after my departure in April 2015; it therefore documents the shared workflow without implying that I created every photograph or document.

**Skills demonstrated:** digital asset organization, image preparation, practical technology adoption, and support for detailed remote examination of physical objects.
